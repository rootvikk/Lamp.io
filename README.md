# lamp.io

**lamp.io** is a minimalist 2D platformer developed in Unity. Step into the shadows as a glowing yellow cube on a mission to find the light. Navigate through stark, dark environments, relying on your platforming skills to cross gaps and reach safety.

Your ultimate goal? The glowing green lamp at the end of each stage. But the path isn't just about surviving—it's about shining brighter by managing your **AURA**.

## 🎮 Play the Game

You can download and play the game on [Itch.io](https://rootvikk.itch.io/lampio) 

## ✨ Features

* **Classic 2D Platforming:** Master simple but precise controls to move, jump, and clear hazards. Powered by responsive physics-based movement and Rigidbody collision detection.
* **Dynamic Lighting & Aesthetics:** Experience a striking, high-contrast visual style where every lamp and shadow pops. Built using dynamic 2D lighting and shadow casting via Unity's Universal Render Pipeline (URP).
* **Build Your Aura:** Collect warm, white lamps to increase your score and boost your Aura.
* **Avoid the Red:** Beware of the ominous red lamps. Touching them will drain your Aura and dim your progress.
* **Event-Driven Visuals:** Features a custom event-driven system that links player stats (Aura) directly to real-time visual game effects.

## ⌨️ Controls

* **Move:** `[A]` / `[D]` or `[Left Arrow]` / `[Right Arrow]`
* **Jump:** `[Spacebar]` or `[Up Arrow]`

## 🛠️ Technical Details

* **Engine:** Unity
* **Language:** C#
* **Graphics:** Universal Render Pipeline (URP) for optimized 2D dynamic lighting.
* **Architecture:** Event-driven architecture for stat-to-visual feedback loops.
